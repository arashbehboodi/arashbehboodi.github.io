Arash Behboodi

I am a research scientist and Director of Engineering at Qualcomm AI Research, working on the theoretical foundations of information and computational systems.

My interests include information theory, learning theory, machine learning for inverse problems, compressed sensing, geometric deep learning, differentiable simulation, neural reasoning and agentic AI.
